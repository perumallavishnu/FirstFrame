Learn photography on a real camera, in your browser

FirstFrame is a free website for learning how a camera works before you buy one. Practise on a 3D Canon EOS R100, take simulated photos and find out why each one came out the way it did.

About FirstFrame

What is FirstFrame?

FirstFrame teaches camera basics with a working model of the Canon EOS R100, a popular first mirrorless camera. The 3D model is built from Canon's product photos and published measurements, so its buttons, doors and ports sit where they do on the real camera. You can press the controls, open the battery door, change lenses and shoot practice photos in a simulator that explains why a picture turned out bright, dark, sharp or blurred.

What can I learn here?

  • How aperture, shutter speed and ISO work together to set exposure
  • What the P, Tv, Av and M modes do, and when to leave Auto
  • Focus, depth of field and a blurred background
  • Metering and how to read a histogram
  • White balance, picture styles, and RAW versus JPEG
  • Focal length, crop factor and which lens suits which subject
  • Every button, dial and port on the camera, by Canon's own names
  • How the battery and SD card go in, and how an external flash mounts

Who is it for?

It is for anyone thinking about buying a first camera who wants to understand it before spending money. It also helps people who own a camera but find the buttons and menus confusing, and teachers who want a free practice tool that students can open without an account.

Do I need a camera or an account?

No. Everything runs in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, with nothing to install and no sign-up. Your lesson progress and practice photos stay in your own browser.

How should a beginner start?

  1. Take the guided tour of the 3D camera to learn what each control is called.
  2. Read the first three lessons on light and the exposure triangle.
  3. Open the simulator in Av mode and compare a portrait at f/4.5 and f/11.
  4. Try the missions for a frozen fan, a soft background and candlelight.
  5. Compare the ten lenses to see whether the kit lens is enough for you.

Is this made by Canon?

No. FirstFrame is an independent learning project and is not affiliated with Canon Inc. Camera and lens names describe real equipment, and each part name and specification links to Canon's manual. The simulator follows real optics closely enough to learn from, but it is a practice tool and not a replacement for the camera itself.
