## Hot shoe (ISO 518)
- Shoe opening about 18.6 mm wide, with rails about 2 mm high. The centre contact is the X-sync; the manufacturer adds extra pins behind it (Canon 4 + 1, Sony Multi Interface 21 pins).
- Model the flash foot to match the camera's own shoe.

- 1/4"-20 UNC, about 6.35 mm thread major diameter, depth about 5 mm. It usually sits on the lens axis; verify in the bottom photo.
